About me

I'm a second-year engineering student at Manipal Institute of Technology, drawn to the space where computational thinking meets economic reasoning. I approach problems first-principles style: define the objective function, identify the constraints, and only then reach for a tool.

My curiosity sits at an interdisciplinary intersection. In class I work through algorithmic design, data abstraction and systems thinking. Outside it, I engage with macroeconomic discourse at the Economics Society of Manipal, sharpen my hypothesis-driven problem-solving through structured case work at the Consulting Club of Manipal, and examine market microstructure and capital allocation as a member of Finova, the campus finance and investment club.

What motivates me is rigor with empathy: building solutions that are analytically sound, communicated clearly, and genuinely useful to the people who rely on them. I value intellectual humility, collaborative inquiry, and the habit of questioning my own assumptions before anyone else has to.
